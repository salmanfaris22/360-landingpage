## Do not

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Buying links, reviews or followers | Spam policy; risk of suspension |
| Keywords in the Business Profile name | Against Google's guidelines |
| Fake review or rating schema | Manual action risk |
| More "best X in Y" or city pages | Scaled-content risk; 69 URLs is already a lot for a young domain |
